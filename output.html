# One-Day Marathon Prep Meal Plan
**For: 180cm, 72kg Vegetarian Athlete**

---

## Daily Nutrition Overview
- **Estimated Calorie Needs:** 2,800-3,000 kcal
- **Carbohydrates:** 60-65% (420-490g) – prioritized around training
- **Protein:** 15-20% (105-150g)
- **Fat:** 20-25% (60-85g)

---

## BREAKFAST (6:30 AM)
*Glycogen replenishment + sustained energy*

- **2 cups cooked oatmeal** with cinnamon
- **1 large banana** (sliced)
- **½ cup blueberries**
- **2 tbsp honey**
- **1 cup low-fat Greek yogurt**
- **1 slice whole grain toast** with 1 tbsp almond butter

**Nutrition:** 650 cal | 115g carbs | 18g protein | 8g fat

---

## PRE-TRAINING SNACK (10:00 AM)
*Easily digestible fuel – consume 60-90 min before run*

- **1 large banana**
- **1 bagel** (white or whole wheat) with 1 tbsp jam
- **200ml sports drink** or coconut water

**Nutrition:** 400 cal | 95g carbs | 7g protein | 1g fat

---

## DURING 2-HOUR RUN (11:30 AM-1:30 PM)
*Fuel every 30-45 minutes*

- **Sports drink:** 500-750ml (6-8% carbohydrate solution)
- **Energy gels:** 2 packets (or homemade: dates + water)
- **Water:** 500-750ml

**Nutrition:** 200 cal | 50g carbs | minimal protein/fat

---

## POST-TRAINING MEAL (1:45 PM)
*Rapid recovery window – carbs + protein within 30 min*

- **1.5 cups white rice** or **2 slices white bread**
- **1 cup lentil soup** or **200g tofu scramble**
- **Large mixed salad** (tomato, cucumber, spinach)
- **1 tbsp olive oil dressing**
- **Electrolyte drink:** 200ml

**Nutrition:** 650 cal | 110g carbs | 22g protein | 10g fat

---

## LUNCH/AFTERNOON SNACK (3:30 PM)

- **2 slices whole grain bread**
- **200g hummus** + grated carrot
- **1 medium apple**
- **1 cup chamomile tea**

**Nutrition:** 350 cal | 65g carbs | 10g protein | 8g fat

---

## DINNER (6:30 PM)
*Balanced, easy to digest*

- **1.5 cups cooked pasta** (white preferred for easier digestion)
- **1 cup marinara sauce** with sautéed vegetables (zucchini, bell peppers)
- **1 cup chickpeas**
- **Side: Steamed broccoli** with lemon
- **Sparkling water** with lemon

**Nutrition:** 650 cal | 120g carbs | 22g protein | 8g fat

---

## EVENING SNACK (8:30 PM)
*Optional – if hungry; aids overnight recovery*

- **1 cup low-fat Greek yogurt**
- **½ cup granola** (nut-free)
- **½ cup berries**

**Nutrition:** 200 cal | 35g carbs | 15g protein | 3g fat

---

## HYDRATION STRATEGY
- **Morning:** 500ml water
- **2 hours pre-run:** 400ml water
- **During run:** 500-750ml sports drink + 500-750ml water
- **Post-run:** 150% of weight lost (typically 750-1000ml)
- **Throughout day:** 3-4 liters total
