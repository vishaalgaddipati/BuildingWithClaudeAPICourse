# One-Day Marathon Prep Meal Plan for Vegetarian Athlete

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete preparing for marathon race day with strategic nutrition timing around a 2-hour training run:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,100 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (110g), Fat (75g), Carbs (420g)

**Meal Plan:**

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM - 3 hours before run):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), sunflower seeds (20g), and honey (15g). Whole milk (240g).
  * Protein: 15g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Pre-Run Snack (8:30 AM - 30 minutes before run):** White bread toast (50g) with jam (20g) and a glass of orange juice (250g).
  * Protein: 3g, Fat: 1g, Carbs: 50g

* **Post-Run Recovery (11:00 AM - within 30 minutes of finishing):** Chocolate milk (500g) with a granola bar (40g, nut-free).
  * Protein: 18g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 70g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Lentil and vegetable soup (350g), whole wheat pita bread (80g), hummus (40g), and mixed green salad with olive oil dressing (30g).
  * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 85g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g, low-fat) with granola (40g) and berries (100g).
  * Protein: 20g, Fat: 5g, Carbs: 65g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Vegetarian pasta with marinara sauce (pasta 120g dry, sauce 150g), chickpeas (100g), steamed zucchini (150g), and parmesan cheese (20g).
  * Protein: 22g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 75g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Rice cakes (30g) with peanut seed butter (25g) and a pear (150g).
  * Protein: 14g, Fat: 19g, Carbs: 55g

This meal plan prioritizes carbohydrates for glycogen loading and includes strategic timing around your 2-hour training run. The post-run recovery snack provides quick carbs and protein to replenish energy stores. All foods are vegetarian and nut-free.
