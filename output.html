# Marathon Training Nutrition Plan

## Pre-Run (2-3 hours before)

**Meal: 60-80g carbs + moderate protein, low fat/fiber**
- Oatmeal (1.5 cups cooked) with banana and honey
- OR: 2 slices toast with jam + glass of orange juice
- Small yogurt with granola

*Why: Digests well, provides steady energy without GI distress*

## During Run (2-hour duration)

**Every 30-45 minutes:**
- Sports drink (6-8% carbs): 200-300ml
- Energy gel with water
- OR: Dates, pretzels, or sports bar

*Target: 30-60g carbs/hour to maintain blood sugar*

## Post-Run (Within 30 minutes)

**Recovery: 40-50g carbs + 15-20g protein**
- Chocolate milk (500ml)
- Toast with peanut butter + banana *(swap for tahini if peanut feels too heavy)*
- Smoothie: banana, yogurt, honey, berries

*Why: Replenishes glycogen while muscles are primed*

## General Daily Nutrition (Marathon Training)

**Carb targets: 6-10g per kg body weight = 430-720g daily**

**Sample day:**
- Breakfast: Oats, toast, fruit
- Snack: Rice cakes, banana
- Lunch: Pasta with lentil bolognese
- Snack: Dried fruit, crackers
- Dinner: Rice/quinoa + beans + vegetables

## Key Notes
- Hydrate consistently (2-3L daily minimum)
- Test all race-day foods during training runs
- Prioritize simple carbs 3 days before race
