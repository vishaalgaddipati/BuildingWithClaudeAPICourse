# One-Day Marathon Prep Meal Plan

## MEAL SCHEDULE

### **BREAKFAST** (7:00 AM) - Pre-Training Fuel
*Calories: 680 | Carbs: 95g | Protein: 22g | Fat: 18g*

- Oatmeal (dry): 80g
- Banana: 150g
- Greek yogurt (plain): 200g
- Honey: 30g
- Sunflower seeds: 20g
- Water: 250ml

**Prep:** Cook oatmeal, top with yogurt, banana slices, honey, and seeds

---

### **TRAINING RUN** (8:30 AM - 10:30 AM)
*During run (after 60 minutes):*
- Sports drink or diluted fruit juice: 250ml
- Banana: 100g

---

### **POST-RUN RECOVERY SNACK** (10:45 AM) - Critical Window
*Calories: 420 | Carbs: 78g | Protein: 14g | Fat: 8g*

- White bread: 80g
- Peanut butter alternative (tahini): 25g

**Prep:** Toast bread, spread tahini and jam, drink juice immediately
